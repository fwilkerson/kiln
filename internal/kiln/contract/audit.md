## kiln's contract

kiln runs this audit. The lines below are kiln's, and hold whatever the text above says.

Read the directories kiln gives you, and the spec it names, `.kiln/specs/%[1]s.md`, with its amendments in
`.kiln/specs/%[1]s.amend.md`, if there are any. A directory means its own files, not its subdirectories. You change
nothing: kiln puts back any file you change.
